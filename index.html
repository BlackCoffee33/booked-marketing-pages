Before you start

Questions? Straight answers.

Do I need a large social following?

No. A following can help organic reach, but paid social puts the right offer in front of the right people whether you have 200 followers or 200,000.

How much ad spend makes sense?

It depends on your revenue, margins, industry, and capacity. We start with the economics: what a customer is worth, what it costs to deliver, and how many more customers you can actually serve. Then we recommend a spend level designed to create more profit, not just more traffic.

Do I keep ownership of my ad accounts and data?

Yes. Your ad accounts, data, Pixel, and audiences stay in your name. We work through permissions you control and can revoke at any time.

What happens after I submit the questionnaire?

We review your answers and build your personalized Blueprint around your capacity, offer, and growth opportunity. You get clear next moves. If there is a strong fit for a Growth Advisory, you hear directly from the founder.

Do I have to create the content myself?

No. We use the strongest content you already have and create additional content as part of our retainer. You bring the business; we build and improve the creative.

How quickly will I know if it is working?

Paid social improves as data accumulates, which is why BOOKED has a 3-month minimum retainer. Content, targeting, your offer, website, and sales process all matter. We identify what works early, then keep iterating to improve the return over time.
